About IASSW

IASSW is the global voice for social work education, committed to excellence in education, social justice and global collaboration.

WHO WE ARE

A Global Voice for Social Work

The International Association of Schools of Social Work (IASSW) is a worldwide network of social work educational institutions, individuals and organizations. We connect and strengthen schools, educators, researchers and practitioners to advance knowledge, build capacity and promote social justice globally.

Governance & Leadership

IASSW is guided by a dedicated leadership team and a robust governance structure that ensures transparency, accountability and strategic direction. Our leadership works collaboratively to advance the organisation’s mission, strengthen international partnerships and support the development of social work education, research and practice. Through effective governance, IASSW brings together leaders and representatives from different regions to promote collaboration, shared responsibility and meaningful global engagement. This structure helps ensure that the organisation remains responsive to the evolving needs of the social work profession and the communities it serves.

IASSW Secretariat

The IASSW Secretariat provides essential operational and administrative support to the organisation, helping coordinate activities, programmes and communications across its global network.

The Secretariat works closely with the leadership, members, committees and regional representatives to ensure effective coordination and smooth implementation of IASSW initiatives. It also supports communication, membership engagement, meetings, events and day-to-day organisational activities.

IASSW’s committees, taskforces and Special Interest Groups (SIGs) bring together experts and members to work on key issues, drive innovation and support the profession’s development.
